# COSMOS
Explore the archive. Track the station. Watch the Sun.

COSMOS brings three live space experiences into one interactive app. Follow the International Space Station and the people aboard it, browse NASA’s image library and 30 years of the Astronomy Picture of the Day, and check today’s space weather to see whether the northern lights might be out tonight. It makes real space data approachable for students, educators and curious explorers by pairing live feeds and NASA imagery with interactive maps, charts and plain-English explanations.
