# COSMOS
Explore the archive.Track the station.
NASA Cosmos Dashboard brings three space experiences into one interactive app. Browse and filter NASA’s image library, follow the ISS and its crew, then plan a trip to destinations across the solar system. It makes space data approachable for students, educators, and curious explorers by pairing live information and NASA imagery with interactive maps, mission visuals, and clear explanations.
What you can explore
- NASA Image Explorer: Search NASA’s image library, filter by year and center, and open image details and download options.
- Live Space Tracker: View the ISS position and orbit track, see who is in space, and estimate how far the station is from your location.
- Space Flight Simulator: Compare destinations, visualize a simulated journey, and explore travel time, launch windows, Δv, propellant estimates, crew supplies, and real mission comparisons.
Why it’s useful
It connects space facts to things you can explore yourself: where the station is, what NASA has photographed, and how mission choices affect a trip. It’s designed as an educational explorer, with assumptions shown alongside simplified orbital calculations.

Built with: Python, Streamlit, PyDeck, and public space-data APIs.
